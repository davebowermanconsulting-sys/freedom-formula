## 1. Purpose
A warm, authentic film in Saretta's own words that explains **what Malang is, why it exists and how people can work with it**. It will be used:
- as the **hero video on the Malang homepage** (silent loop plus full film with sound);
- on social media, in grant applications, in partner and sponsor proposals, and at events.

**Single message:** *Malang (meaning "together") creates opportunities through culture, connecting Aboriginal artists with communities, organisations and audiences.*

## 2. Approach
- **Interview to camera, unscripted answers.** Saretta answers questions in her own words. We don't write a script for her to read, because natural answers are more authentic and easier to deliver. The questions are shaped so the edit can build a clear story.
- A short **optional opening/closing line** for Saretta to say to camera is included (section 5) if she's comfortable. She can change or drop it.
- **B-roll** of artwork, hands at work, artists, workshops and Country, cut over the interview.
- Tone: grounded, proud, hopeful, community-first.

## 3. Cultural protocols & consent (before the shoot)
- [ ] Confirm with Saretta whether the film should open with an **Acknowledgement of Country**, and the exact wording and Country name she wants used.
- [ ] Confirm any artworks, places, stories or people that **should not** be filmed or shown, and any images of people who have passed away.
- [ ] **Talent release** signed by Saretta and anyone else on camera. **Artwork permission** from each artist whose work appears, with name credits in the edit as each artist prefers.
- [ ] Location permission for any public or third-party sites.
- [ ] Saretta approves the final edit before release. Malang retains the right to request removal of any content.

## 4. Interview questions
Ask in this order. Saretta can skip any question. Ask her to include the question in her answer ("Malang means…"), so each answer stands alone in the edit.

**Who & why**
1. Tell us who you are and where you're from.
2. What does the word *Malang* mean, and why did you choose it?
3. Why did you start Malang? What was missing for artists?
4. How has your own journey as an artist shaped Malang?

**What Malang does**
5. In a few sentences, what does Malang do?
6. Tell us about **Artists Together**. What happens for an artist who joins?
7. What does it mean to you to see an artist grow through the program? Can you share a moment that stayed with you? (Use names only with that artist's consent.)
8. How does Malang work with organisations: events and conferences, commissions, workshops, Reconciliation Action Plans?
9. What do organisations gain when they work with Aboriginal artists in a genuine way?

**Looking ahead**
10. What's next for Malang? *(Prompt only if she's comfortable talking about plans: online learning, an art shop for Artists Together artists.)*
11. What would you say to an artist thinking about getting involved?
12. What would you say to a business or organisation thinking about working with Malang?
13. If people remember one thing about Malang, what should it be?

**Pick-ups (short lines for the edit)**
- "Malang means together."
- "Creating opportunities through culture."
- "Local artists, local stories."
- "Come and work with us." / "Get in touch with Malang."

## 5. Optional lines for Saretta to say to camera (her choice)
